From suggestion to adoption
  1. Describe the device, changes and exact source quotations.
  2. Continue to a GitHub Issue, attach actual captures and submit.
  3. Maintainers check model, configuration and scope, and record the decision.
  4. An accepted update is rebuilt into the website and downloads.
What evidence helps?
A matching official page, datasheet or traceable report. Identify the page or table and keep the unhighlighted capture. Third-party PPD is reference-only; adopted PPD is official or calculated by the project.
