01CollectionCapture sources · match configurations · adopt values+

The database adds evidence while maintaining credible support. Devices are reviewed individually across the full catalogue. Complete and incomplete records are shown separately, with an archive entry for complete devices before 2016.

Complete devices before 2016 remain accessible through the device archives, outside the main dataset since 2016; their year alone does not put them in the incomplete dataset. Sufficient, matching official evidence is preferred, with reliable third-party evidence used for official omissions. Failed access, nonofficial sources, estimates and unspecified FOV axes do not automatically remove devices. Unreviewed entries remain pending without a claim of missing data or a passed review. Complete main records have category, year, complete head-worn mass, per-eye pixels, FOV, optics and working PPD. Sourced mass bounds retain their inequalities; they neither exclude devices nor become exact point values automatically. Calibration-reference eligibility is recorded separately. Adopted PPD is official or a project algorithm estimate. Third-party PPD is reference-only; calculation methods are documented in their derivations.

Device catalogue

Scope and conventions

Device categories are Standalone VR, PC VR and AR. Classification follows source descriptions of device type and connections. Phone connectivity does not imply the absence of a built-in display and is insufficient to classify a device as PC VR.

Click any parameter for its evidence. Category and year sit with the device; display type sits with resolution. Weight bounds retain their inequalities.
